Questions

Before you enroll

Do I need prior experience to start?

Most tracks assume no prior background — the first two weeks cover fundamentals before projects begin. A few advanced tracks list prerequisites on their syllabus page.

Can I switch tracks after enrolling?

Yes, within the first week of a cohort. Reach out to your mentor or support and we'll move your seat at no extra cost.

Is there a payment plan?

Every course can be paid in three interest-free installments, or in full for a small discount. Details are on each checkout page.

What happens if I fall behind?

Cohorts include recorded sessions and open office hours, so you can catch up without losing your seat. Mentors flag at-risk progress early.

Not ready for a cohort? Start self-paced, upgrade anytime.
