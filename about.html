About Stackly

We built the school we wished existed.

Stackly started as a Saturday study group of six self-taught developers frustrated with courses that ended at "here's a video." Today it's a studio-style academy with mentors pulled straight from the companies we're training people to join.

Questions

Before you apply.

Do I need a coding background to apply?

No. About half of each cohort starts from scratch. What matters more in the interview is how you talk through a problem you don't know the answer to yet.

How much time does a cohort actually take?

Plan on 20-25 hours a week for 12 weeks, split between project work, mentor sessions, and cohort critique. Most people keep a part-time job alongside it.

What happens if I don't land a job?

Career services keeps working your search after graduation with no cutoff date. You keep mentor access and interview prep until you're placed.

Are cohorts fully remote?

Yes, cohorts run fully remote with mentors and classmates across 12 countries. Live sessions are scheduled across overlapping time zones and always recorded.

Curious what a cohort feels like?

Sit in on a live studio session, free, no application required.
